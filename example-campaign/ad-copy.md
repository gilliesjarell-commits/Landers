# Ad Copy: Home Warranty (Meta Ads)

Traffic source: **Meta (Facebook + Instagram)**, one ad set per angle, $50 split equally (~$16.67 each), each pointing at its own page.
Headlines are 40 characters max and descriptions 30 max (checked).

**Before launch:** start with no Special Ad Category. If Meta rejects or flags an ad, re-launch that campaign under the category it names (Financial Products and Services or Housing); that removes age, gender and ZIP targeting. Keep ads free of personal attributes: describe a scenario, never assert facts about the viewer (their home's age, their finances).

Placeholder affiliate link in use: `https://example.com/offer`. Swap it with `/swap-link home-warranty-2 <link>`.

## Pain Point (Advertorial)

**Landing page:** https://chanelgit.github.io/landers/home-warranty-2/pain.html

### Hooks

1. The AC never quits on a mild day. Here's how to be ready.
2. Something in the house is going to break. Make it a phone call.
3. One broken water heater can upend a whole week. There's a calmer way.

### PAS body

**Pain:** Systems in a house wear out on their own schedule: the AC in a heat wave, the water heater on a workday morning.
**Agitate:** Then comes the scramble: calling around, waiting days, taking time off, and saying yes to a quote you can't judge. Homeowners insurance generally doesn't pay for things that simply wear out.
**Solution:** A home warranty plan gives you one number to call and a set service fee per visit for covered breakdowns. See what a plan would cover for your home. (Coverage varies by plan and state.)

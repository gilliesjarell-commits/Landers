# Ad copy: Vehicle-Secured Credit Card (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook)

Rules followed: no personal-attribute wording ("Bad credit?", "Were you denied?"), no approval promises,
no "no credit check", no APR, limit or fee numbers, no fake urgency, no first-person customer stories.
Meta ads run under the Financial Products and Services special ad category. Meta and TikTok use the same
primary texts, headlines and descriptions.

---

## Pain Point → Advertorial
**Lands on:** https://gilliesjarell-commits.github.io/Landers/car-credit-card/pain.html

### Hooks
1. The repair bill came in. The credit card application didn't go through.
2. A secured card that doesn't ask for a cash deposit.
3. The car in your driveway might help you get a credit card.

### PAS body
A surprise repair or bill shows up, and the usual options aren't there: regular cards say no, and secured
cards want a cash deposit. A vehicle-secured credit card uses your car's value instead of a deposit to help
set the credit line, and you keep driving the car. It's secured by the vehicle, so it's for people who can
make the payments. See how it works and whether it's available where you live.

### Facebook / Instagram / TikTok
**Primary texts**
1. (Short) A credit card that uses your car's value instead of a cash deposit. See how it works.
2. (Medium) Secured cards usually want a cash deposit up front. This one uses your vehicle's value instead, and you keep driving your car. Subject to approval; not available in all states. See how it works.
3. (Story) The mechanic calls with a number you weren't ready for. You try a card application on your lunch break, and it doesn't go through. Then you look at the keys on the table. Some credit cards now use your car's value, not a cash deposit, to help set a credit line. See how it works.

**Headlines (≤40)**
1. Your car could help you get a card
2. A secured card with no cash deposit
3. Use your car's value, keep driving it

**Descriptions (≤30)**
1. See how it works
2. Subject to approval

CAMPAIGN AGENT for Claude Code
==============================
Open "Campaign-Agent-Setup-Guide.pdf" and follow it step by step.

What's in this folder:
  _config.yml                        Keeps your system files off your live site
  CLAUDE.md                          The rules and your settings. Claude reads it automatically.
  RUNBOOK.md                         A short reference for every command and feature
  .claude/commands/setup.md          /setup         one-time setup
  .claude/commands/launch-offer.md   /launch-offer  build and deploy a campaign
  .claude/commands/swap-link.md      /swap-link     change the affiliate link on a campaign
  .claude/commands/swap-logo.md      /swap-logo     rename the publisher / swap the logo
  .claude/commands/swap-image.md     /swap-image    replace or regenerate images and image ads
  .claude/commands/models.md         /models        choose the image and video models (muapi.ai)
  .claude/commands/legal.md          /legal         fill the Privacy Policy and Terms, update disclaimers
  templates/                         Advertorial, Direct, Quiz, dashboard, Privacy and Terms templates
  _tools/                            The image-ad builder (used automatically)
  _addons/video-ads/                 Optional video ads add-on, switched off (see page 11 of the guide)
  example-campaign/                  A finished example (home warranty, with images and image ads)

Note: the .claude folder can be hidden.
  Mac: in Finder press Cmd + Shift + . (period) to see it.
  Windows: unzip first (right-click > Extract All), then in File Explorer turn on View > Show > Hidden items.

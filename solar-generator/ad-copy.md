# Ad copy: Solar Generator (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook)

Rules followed: no runtime or savings numbers, no "never lose power" promises, no fake urgency, no
first-person customer stories (story-style texts are second-person scenarios), no claims the page
doesn't make. Meta and TikTok use the same primary texts, headlines and descriptions.

---

## Pain Point → Advertorial
**Lands on:** https://gilliesjarell-commits.github.io/Landers/solar-generator/pain.html

### Hooks
1. The power's out. The fridge is quiet. Now what?
2. Your phone's at 20% and the outage map says "unknown."
3. Still dragging out the gas generator every storm?

### PAS body
The lights go out and the clock starts on everything in the fridge. Phones drain, the Wi-Fi's gone, and
the only backup in the garage is a loud gas generator that has to run outside on fuel you may not have.
A solar generator is a quieter plan: a battery you charge ahead of time, plug the essentials into, and
top back up with sunlight. See how it works and whether it fits your home.

3. (Story) The lights flicker twice and then they're gone. You find the flashlight in the third drawer you check. Down the street a generator roars to life, and you think about the stale gas can in your garage. There's a quieter way to keep the basics running. See how it works.
